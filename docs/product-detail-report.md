## 1. Mobile Banking

### What it is

A digital banking and wallet application for retail users, covering payments, transfers, bill pay, top-up, cards, loans, and agent-banking access.

### Main problems solved

- branch dependency for basic transactions
- fragmented payment experiences
- low engagement after account opening
- weak cross-sell into savings, credit, insurance, and merchant payments

### Target users

- banked and underbanked consumers
- gig workers and salary earners
- migrants and remittance receivers
- rural customers using agents

### Core capabilities

- account view and transaction history
- cash-in / cash-out
- local transfers and wallet transfers
- bill payment and top-up
- loan offers and virtual cards
- agent-assisted servicing

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- World Bank says account ownership reached `71%` of adults in developing economies by 2021, and digital payments keep driving usage.
- In Sub-Saharan Africa, mobile money remains a major financial-inclusion driver.
- In Asia, digital merchant payment adoption accelerated sharply during and after the pandemic, creating more reasons to keep customers active in-app.

### Business model

- transaction fees
- interchange / card-related revenue
- commissions from billers and partners
- cross-sell into loans, savings, insurance, and merchant acquiring

### Key success factors

- trusted UX and low failed transactions
- strong local payment rails and biller integrations
- fraud controls
- agent, merchant, and wallet ecosystem density

### Competitor examples

- Asia: Maya, Tonik, GCash, TrueMoney, K Plus
- Africa: M-PESA, PalmPay, Opay, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money

### Strategic fit for Wing

This is the front door of the ecosystem. It matters most when paired with merchant acceptance, agent services, and pre-qualified credit.

## 2. eKYC and Video KYC

### What it is

A digital identity-verification platform for remote onboarding, document verification, liveness detection, face matching, and compliance review.

### Main problems solved

- slow paper-based onboarding
- branch-heavy account opening
- fraud risk in identity verification
- inconsistent compliance across products and channels

### Target users

- banks
- fintechs
- lenders
- insurers
- telcos
- marketplaces needing seller verification

### Core capabilities

- ID capture and OCR
- liveness check
- face match
- video KYC sessions
- sanctions / watchlist integration
- audit logs and case management

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- remote onboarding is now table stakes for banks and fintechs launching digital products
- regulated sectors increasingly need reusable identity, consent, and audit trails
- cross-border and multi-channel onboarding creates operational complexity that manual teams cannot scale well

### Business model

- per verification fee
- monthly platform fee
- enterprise integration and workflow fee

### Key success factors

- document coverage by country
- high pass rates with low fraud leakage
- regulator comfort and auditability
- fast API and low-friction mobile UX

### Competitor examples

- Asia: ADVANCE.AI, Sumsub, Tookitaki partners, local RegTech vendors
- Africa: Smile Identity, VerifyMe, Seamfix, enterprise KYC integrators

### Strategic fit for Wing

This is a shared capability across mobile banking, merchant onboarding, BNPL, agri onboarding, and LOS. It should be positioned as a reusable trust layer, not just a standalone product.

## 3. Digital Loans

### What it is

A digital lending experience for instant or near-instant personal and SME loans, using transaction behavior, rule-based scoring, and automated approval flows.

### Main problems solved

- long turnaround times for small loans
- low access to formal credit for SMEs and mass-market users
- weak use of transaction data in credit underwriting
- poor customer experience in document-heavy lending

### Target users

- salaried consumers
- wallet users
- merchants
- micro and small businesses
- farmers and informal earners transitioning into formal credit

### Core capabilities

- digital application
- internal scoring and policy rules
- alternative-data underwriting
- disbursement into wallet or account
- repayment reminders and collections support

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- IFC highlights a very large formal SME finance gap across Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa.
- Embedded credit works especially well when payments data already exists.
- In emerging markets, many borrowers are credit-thin but transaction-rich.

### Business model

- interest income
- origination fees
- servicing fees
- revenue share with funding partners

### Key success factors

- good risk segmentation
- clean repayment rails
- careful fraud controls
- disciplined collections
- tight integration with merchant and wallet data

### Competitor examples

- Asia: Kredivo, Akulaku, Atome, local bank digital lenders
- Africa: Branch, FairMoney, M-KOPA financing products, Moniepoint credit

### Strategic fit for Wing

This should be built on payment and operational data from the wallet, merchant app, and agri ecosystem. That data advantage is the main moat.

## 4. Loyalty Platform

### What it is

A rewards and gamified engagement platform, including the "Surprise Box" concept, to drive repeat purchase, higher activity, and stronger partner-brand engagement.

### Main problems solved

- low repeat usage after acquisition
- limited consumer engagement with financial apps
- weak cross-brand reward interoperability
- high campaign execution overhead for marketing teams

### Target users

- banks and wallets
- large merchant groups
- FMCG brands
- retail networks

### Core capabilities

- points and reward rules
- instant-win campaigns
- coupon and voucher management
- cross-brand campaigns
- segmentation and analytics

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- customer acquisition costs are rising
- wallets and merchants need habit loops, not just first-time installs
- loyalty can strengthen ecosystem usage across payments, shopping, and credit

### Business model

- SaaS subscription
- campaign setup fees
- reward-redemption and partner participation fees

### Key success factors

- simple mechanics for users
- measurable uplift in frequency and retention
- partner network scale
- campaign ROI visibility

### Competitor examples

- Asia: Capillary, Antsomi partners, retail loyalty SaaS vendors
- Africa: local retail loyalty operators, bank reward platforms, merchant coupon engines

### Strategic fit for Wing

This is best used as an ecosystem amplifier for mobile banking, merchant app, and marketplace products rather than as a standalone hero product.

## 5. Merchant App

### What it is

A merchant operating app covering onboarding, payment acceptance, outlet management, cashier control, earnings visibility, product catalog, and access to working capital.

### Main problems solved

- merchants accept digital payments but still reconcile manually
- fragmented tools for payments, staff, and inventory
- poor access to same-day business insight
- limited access to formal working capital

### Target users

- SME merchants
- multi-outlet retail
- food and beverage stores
- service merchants
- social sellers graduating into formal commerce

### Core capabilities

- self-onboarding
- QR / wallet acceptance
- transaction history and settlement view
- cashier management
- product and order management
- sales analytics
- loan offers based on merchant activity

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- ADB shows Asia's MSMEs are heavily concentrated in services and trade, which maps directly to merchant tooling demand.
- GSMA reports merchant mobile-money payments surpassed `$100B` globally in 2024, with Africa contributing the majority by value and Asia growing quickly.
- Many SME merchants still need a simple "one app" operating layer, not a complex enterprise suite.

### Business model

- merchant service fees
- subscription for premium dashboards or tools
- embedded-finance revenue
- payment and settlement fees

### Key success factors

- frictionless onboarding
- stable settlements
- useful daily reporting
- strong offline / low-bandwidth behavior
- compelling access to credit

### Competitor examples

- Asia: HitPay, Xendit SME flows, DOKU merchant tools, FOMO Pay
- Africa: Yoco, Moniepoint, Paymob, Flutterwave Store / POS offers

### Strategic fit for Wing

This is one of the strongest products in the portfolio because it connects payments, operations, and lending in a way that is highly relevant for emerging-market SMEs.

## 6. eCommerce Suite

### What it is

A family of commerce products, including marketplace, quick commerce, shopping, and logistics orchestration. In the deck this is represented by WingMall, WingExpress, and WingShopping.

### Main problems solved

- limited digital storefront capability for merchants
- weak last-mile coordination
- fragmented buyer, seller, and rider experiences
- low conversion without integrated payments and financing

### Target users

- buyers / consumers
- merchants / sellers
- riders / logistics partners
- marketplace operator

### Core capabilities

- catalog and storefront management
- order capture
- basket and checkout
- logistics dispatch and tracking
- seller operations and payouts
- integrated payment and BNPL options

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- commerce growth continues, but profitability depends on better logistics and payments integration
- verified seller networks and embedded finance can improve trust and conversion
- ecosystem-led commerce can anchor merchant acquisition for payments

### Business model

- take rate on GMV
- delivery fees
- ads / sponsored listings
- merchant subscriptions
- embedded payment and credit monetization

### Key success factors

- dense local logistics
- good merchant supply
- high repeat frequency
- low failed deliveries
- strong checkout options

### Competitor examples

- Asia: GrabMart, Shopee ecosystem services, Tokopedia seller tools, Lazada ecosystem
- Africa: Jumia ecosystem, Glovo merchant tooling, local grocery-commerce operators

### Strategic fit for Wing

This product is strongest when focused on curated categories or ecosystem commerce rather than broad "everything store" competition.

## 7. Buy Now Pay Later

### What it is

A checkout financing layer that lets customers split payments while merchants receive faster conversion and higher basket size.

### Main problems solved

- purchase abandonment for mid-ticket items
- low affordability at checkout
- limited access to short-term formal credit

### Target users

- consumers shopping online or in store
- merchants seeking higher conversion
- banks and lenders seeking point-of-sale credit growth

### Core capabilities

- instant eligibility check
- embedded checkout offer
- installment plan management
- merchant settlement
- repayment reminders and collections

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- BNPL works best where merchants want higher average order value and customers want predictable short-term financing
- it is particularly effective when integrated into merchant apps and marketplaces rather than sold in isolation
- it can act as a bridge product before borrowers graduate to larger personal or SME loans

### Business model

- merchant discount rate
- consumer late fees where permitted
- interest or financing revenue depending on structure

### Key success factors

- fraud and credit control
- clear underwriting
- transparent user communication
- merchant adoption at checkout

### Competitor examples

- Asia: Atome, Kredivo, Akulaku, SPayLater-style regional models
- Africa: Payflex, Float, local installment players, bank-led POS finance

### Strategic fit for Wing

BNPL should be tightly coupled with merchant acceptance and marketplace checkout. Without merchant distribution, it becomes expensive to grow.

## 8. WingAgri

### What it is

A digital agriculture marketplace and finance platform connecting farmers, cooperatives, and buyers with trade tools, supply-chain finance, and farm management insight.

### Main problems solved

- fragmented market access for farmers
- weak price discovery
- delayed payments and payout complexity
- poor access to working capital
- limited visibility over farm cycles, stock, and buyers

### Target users

- farmers
- cooperatives
- aggregators
- institutional buyers
- agri-input and finance partners

### Core capabilities

- farmer and buyer onboarding
- produce listing and sourcing
- cooperative stock and payout management
- loan application and disbursement
- farm cycle and weather-linked tracking

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- agriculture still supports large populations and incomes across both regions
- digitized trade plus finance can reduce leakage and strengthen rural inclusion
- cooperative-led models can scale faster than purely farmer-direct models

### Business model

- transaction fee
- financing revenue
- subscription or service fees for cooperatives / buyers
- optional data or value-added advisory services

### Key success factors

- trusted field onboarding
- buyer liquidity
- strong payout rails
- localized workflow for crop cycles and cooperative processes

### Competitor examples

- Asia: DeHaat-style agri platforms, agribusiness marketplaces, cooperative digitization platforms
- Africa: Twiga, Apollo Agriculture adjacencies, agri-finance and sourcing networks

### Strategic fit for Wing

This product can be strategically powerful because it links financial inclusion, trade, and lending in a sector where traditional financial infrastructure is still weak.

## 9. Loan Origination System

### What it is

An enterprise lending workflow platform that manages application intake, documents, credit assessment, approval routing, compliance, and disbursement handoff.

### Main problems solved

- fragmented loan processing
- slow approvals
- inconsistent credit decisions
- weak audit and policy enforcement
- poor visibility across underwriting steps

### Target users

- banks
- microfinance institutions
- consumer finance companies
- SME lenders

### Core capabilities

- application intake
- document management
- scoring and policy rules
- workflow and approval routing
- KYC / AML controls
- offer generation and disbursement handoff

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- lenders across both regions still rely on email, spreadsheets, and branch-led processing for many products
- LOS becomes more valuable when it is configurable by product, channel, and country policy
- reusable LOS capability supports personal, vehicle, mortgage, and SME products

### Business model

- license / SaaS fees
- implementation and customization revenue
- support and maintenance

### Key success factors

- configurability
- integration depth
- fast implementation
- high auditability
- strong credit-policy governance

### Competitor examples

- Asia and Africa: Newgen, Finastra components, local LOS vendors, bank-built internal systems

### Strategic fit for Wing

LOS is a B2B enterprise product. It can strengthen Wing's credibility with banks, but sales cycles will be longer than consumer or merchant products.

## 10. ERP System

### What it is

A multi-module enterprise resource planning platform covering finance, inventory, procurement, HR, CRM, bank transactions, reporting, and workflow automation.

### Main problems solved

- disconnected back-office systems
- poor financial visibility
- weak procurement and inventory control
- inconsistent reporting across business units

### Target users

- mid-size enterprises
- conglomerate subsidiaries
- multi-branch businesses
- organizations needing local customization

### Core capabilities

- finance and accounting
- AP / AR
- inventory and warehouse
- procurement
- employee expense and travel
- HR and payroll
- reporting and dashboards

### Why it matters in Asia and Africa

- many businesses still use a patchwork of spreadsheets plus partial software
- regional ERP demand often favors flexible localization over massive global suites
- there is room for sector-focused ERP when tied to payments, merchant operations, or distribution ecosystems

### Business model

- implementation fee
- license or subscription
- customization and support revenue

### Key success factors

- fast localization
- strong reporting
- practical workflows
- clear migration path from spreadsheet-heavy operations

### Competitor examples

- Asia and Africa: Odoo partners, SAP Business One, Oracle NetSuite, local ERP vendors

### Strategic fit for Wing

ERP is valuable for group synergies and enterprise relationships, but it is less naturally aligned with the payment-led ecosystem than merchant, lending, and onboarding products.

3. Digital Loans

5. Loan Origination System

### Tier B: strong adjacency

1. Buy Now Pay Later

2. Job Portal

## Recommended narrative for investors or partners

The cleanest story is not to present 11 disconnected products. The stronger story is:

1. `Acquire and onboard`
   eKYC, Mobile Banking, Merchant App
2. `Transact and operate`
   Payments, merchant operations, marketplace, agri trade
3. `Underwrite and grow`
   Digital Loans, BNPL, LOS
4. `Retain and expand`
   Loyalty, ERP, data-driven cross-sell

## Immediate recommendation

If you want to sharpen the portfolio commercially, lead with five anchor products:

- Mobile Banking
- Merchant App
- eKYC / Video KYC
- Digital Loans
- Loan Origination System

These five create the clearest ecosystem and the strongest reuse of shared capabilities, data, and distribution.
